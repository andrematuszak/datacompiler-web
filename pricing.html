Frequently asked questions

How is the price calculated?

Per word: transcription starts at €0.02 per word and translation at €0.05 per word, multiplied by a difficulty factor for document type (fiscal, legal, handwriting).

When do I pay?

Pay-as-you-go: you pay when you download the finished document. The exact price is shown before you order.

Is there a subscription or minimum?

No subscription and no minimum — each document is priced individually. A monthly plan for teams is coming soon.

What formats do I receive?

Transcription and translation deliver PDF, text and XLIFF artifacts; the preview shows the first page rendered before you order.
