Translate French documents

Reading time: 3 min

French is one of the most common source languages for document translation: French administrative documents, contracts with French entities, invoices from French suppliers, academic records and official certificates all need English or other-language versions. DataCompiler translates French documents into English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for with French source documents

French documents often contain administrative boilerplate ("Nous soussignés", "Fait à", references to "code civil" articles) that needs correct legal equivalents abroad. Accents and the œ ligature must survive the transcription step — a common OCR failure on older scans. French number/date conventions (comma decimals, "1er", long dates) also need careful handling so amounts and dates stay correct in translation.

Common French documents translated

  • Contracts and commercial agreements (French law is frequent in international trade)
  • Administrative documents: tax notices, official certificates, résumés de situation
  • Invoices and accounting documents
  • Academic diplomas and transcripts (French "grandes écoles" credentials)
  • Notarized documents and powers of attorney
