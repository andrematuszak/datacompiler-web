Translate documents from English to German

Reading time: 3 min

English-to-German is one of the busiest document translation corridors in Europe. German companies expanding internationally need English documents localized for German-speaking markets; conversely, English-language contracts, manuals and certificates from US or UK partners need a proper German version for legal, regulatory or commercial use in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Documents commonly translated EN → DE

  • Contracts and agreements — NDAs, supply contracts, distribution agreements
  • Technical documentation — manuals, datasheets, installation guides, software documentation
  • Certificates and compliance documents — ISO certificates, CE declarations, product conformity
  • Company documents — commercial register extracts, powers of attorney, incorporation papers
  • HR and legal documents — employment contracts, certificates, court documents

What makes German translation different

German is a precision language: compound nouns pack meaning into single words, and legal German has formulaic phrasing ("hiermit", "unterzeichnet") that must match established conventions. Machine translation produces understandable German but often misses the formal register expected in contracts — DataCompiler's human-review step catches the passages where register and terminology matter most.

German is also ~30% longer than English in word count, so text expansion during layout re-typesetting matters for forms and tables.

Scans, photos and handwriting
