Reading time: 3 min

Poland is a major nearshoring and manufacturing hub in Europe, so Polish document translation is in steady demand: employment contracts, company documents (KRS extracts), invoices, certificates and correspondence with Polish subsidiaries all need accurate Polish versions. DataCompiler translates into Polish from English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for in Polish document translation

Polish is a highly inflected Slavic language — seven grammatical cases mean nouns change form depending on their role in the sentence. Machine translation handles this reasonably but produces awkward phrasing when the source structure doesn't map cleanly. Formal documents also use specific formulaic language ("niniejszym", "w nawiązaniu do") that has no literal English equivalent.

Polish uses Latin script with diacritics (ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż) — characters that older tools sometimes drop or mangle. DataCompiler preserves characters such as ł and the euro sign correctly in its rebuilt documents.
