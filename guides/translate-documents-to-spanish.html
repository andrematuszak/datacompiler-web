Translate documents to Spanish

Reading time: 3 min

Spanish is one of the world's most spoken languages and a frequent target for document translation: Latin American and Spanish business partners, immigration paperwork, certificates and contracts all require Spanish versions. DataCompiler translates into Spanish from English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for in Spanish document translation

The biggest question is which Spanish. Neutral Latin American Spanish and European Spanish differ in vocabulary and some grammar — "ordenador" vs "computadora", formal address conventions, date formats. If your document is headed to Spain, a Latin American rendering can look off, and vice versa. Tell the service your target region when it matters.

Spanish also runs longer than English (15–25%), and formal documents require the usted register — informal tú phrasing is inappropriate in contracts and official paperwork.

Common documents translated into Spanish

  • Contracts, NDAs and service agreements
  • Immigration and visa documents, residency paperwork
  • Certificates: birth, marriage, academic, police clearance
  • Invoices, tax documents and financial statements
  • Product documentation and manuals
