Translate Chinese documents

Reading time: 3 min

Chinese documents are increasingly common in international business: contracts with Chinese partners, certificates of origin, academic credentials, government-issued documents and business licenses. DataCompiler translates Chinese (Simplified) documents into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Turkish.

What to watch for with Chinese source documents

The first question is script again: DataCompiler reads Simplified Chinese. Traditional Chinese characters (used in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and older documents) may transcribe poorly — if your document is Traditional, flag it before ordering. Chinese documents also rely heavily on seals (chops), stamps and handwritten annotations, all of which benefit from the human-review step.

Chinese has no spaces between words and no alphabet — word-count pricing is based on the source document, so a dense Chinese page may price differently than expected. Names also follow different conventions (family name first), which matters in legal documents.

Common Chinese documents translated

  • Business licenses and company registration documents
  • Contracts and partnership agreements
  • Certificates of origin and trade documents
  • Academic diplomas and professional qualifications
  • Government-issued certificates and notarized documents

How DataCompiler handles Chinese documents

Upload the file — scans and photos of Chinese documents are common. The pipeline transcribes the Simplified Chinese text, then translates into your chosen target. Preview both versions side by side; flagged passages go to human review, important for names, amounts and official terminology.
