Translate documents to German

Reading time: 3 min

Germany is Europe's largest economy, which makes German one of the most common target languages for business document translation: contracts, technical documentation, certificates and correspondence all need accurate German versions. DataCompiler translates into German from English, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What makes German document translation different

German compounds are the classic challenge: a single concept that takes three English words often becomes one long German noun ("Datenschutzerklärung", "Geschäftsführungsbeschluss"). Machine translation sometimes splits these awkwardly or picks the wrong register. Formal documents also require the correct address form — Sie for business, never du — and legal German has formulaic phrasing where a literal translation reads as foreign.

Like French, German text runs longer than English, roughly 15–25%, which matters when the translation has to fit the original page.

Common documents translated into German

  • Commercial contracts, NDAs and terms of service
  • Technical manuals, datasheets and specifications
  • Certificates: trade register extracts, ISO documents, compliance papers
  • Invoices and financial reports
  • Employment documents and job descriptions
