Translate documents from English to Chinese

Reading time: 3 min

English-to-Chinese is the busiest translation corridor into Asia: companies entering the Chinese market need contracts, certifications and technical documents in Simplified Chinese, and English-language paperwork from international partners constantly needs a Chinese version for Chinese counterparties, regulators and banks.

Documents commonly translated EN → ZH

  • Commercial contracts — supply agreements, distribution deals, NDAs with Chinese partners
  • Certificates and compliance documents — ISO certificates, product conformity, certificates of origin
  • Technical documentation — user manuals, datasheets, installation guides
  • Trade documents — invoices, packing lists, bills of lading
  • Company and legal documents — business licenses, powers of attorney, registration extracts

What makes Chinese translation different

Chinese has no spaces between words and no alphabet, so text behaves very differently from English: a paragraph that fills half a page in English may take a third of a page in Chinese characters. The translated text is re-typeset to fit the original layout — important for contracts and forms.

Names, amounts, dates and legal terms are the highest-risk areas. Passages the engine flags as uncertain go to human review before delivery — this is where a wrong company name or a shifted decimal gets caught.

Simplified vs Traditional: DataCompiler produces Simplified Chinese (mainland China, Singapore). For Taiwan, Hong Kong or Macau, tell us before ordering — see the Chinese document translation overview.

Scans, photos and handwriting
