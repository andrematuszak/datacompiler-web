Translate documents to Chinese

Reading time: 3 min

China remains one of the largest trading partners for European and American businesses, and document translation into Chinese is essential for contracts, certificates, product documentation and correspondence with Chinese partners. DataCompiler translates into Simplified Chinese (the standard used in mainland China) from English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Turkish.

What to watch for in Chinese document translation

The first question is script: Simplified Chinese is used in mainland China and Singapore; Traditional Chinese is used in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau. DataCompiler produces Simplified Chinese — if your document is headed to Taiwan or Hong Kong, say so before ordering, as the script choice affects every character.

Chinese has no spaces between words and no alphabet, so word-count estimates differ from Latin-script languages — pricing is based on the source document's word count. Formulas, codes and Western proper nouns also need consistent handling rules.

Common documents translated into Chinese

  • Commercial contracts and partnership agreements
  • Certificates: ISO, product compliance, certificates of origin
  • Technical documentation and user manuals
  • Invoices and shipping documents
  • Academic diplomas and professional qualifications

How DataCompiler handles Chinese translation

Upload a PDF, scan, photo or handwritten document. The pipeline transcribes the source text first — making scans and handwriting usable — then translates into Simplified Chinese. You preview both versions side by side, and uncertain passages are flagged for human review. That matters for names, numbers, dates and legal terminology.
