Translate documents to Italian

Reading time: 3 min

Italian is a core business language in Europe, and document translation into Italian is common for contracts with Italian partners, legal paperwork, certificates and correspondence. DataCompiler translates into Italian from English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for in Italian document translation

Italian business writing is more formal and formulaic than English: contracts open with set phrases, and the distinction between Lei (formal) and tu (informal) is strict. A translation that slips into informal register in a contract will look amateurish. Italian is also highly inflected — verb endings carry meaning that English expresses with separate words — so word-for-word tools produce stilted output.

Length-wise Italian runs roughly 10–15% longer than English. For layout-sensitive documents, re-typesetting matters as much as the translation itself.

Common documents translated into Italian

  • Commercial contracts and partnership agreements
  • Legal documents: powers of attorney, court filings, compliance papers
  • Certificates and apostilles
  • Invoices and financial statements
  • User manuals and product documentation
