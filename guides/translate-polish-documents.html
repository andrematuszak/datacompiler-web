Poland's position as a nearshoring hub means Polish documents flow into international business constantly: KRS company extracts, employment contracts, invoices, certificates and official correspondence. DataCompiler translates Polish documents into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for with Polish source documents

Polish is a Slavic language with seven grammatical cases — nouns change form by role, so word order matters less than in English and literal translation produces confusion. Diacritics (ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż) are essential and must survive OCR intact. Polish administrative documents are dense with formulaic phrasing and references to specific laws ("art.", "ust.", "pkt") that need correct handling in translation.

Common Polish documents translated

  • Employment contracts and HR paperwork
  • Invoices and financial statements
