Translate documents from Polish to English

Reading time: 3 min

Polish-to-English is one of the most requested translation corridors for companies working with Polish partners, subsidiaries and employees. Polish companies expanding abroad need English versions of corporate, legal and commercial documents; foreign companies hiring in Poland need Polish HR documents translated — and KRS registry extracts circulate internationally in both directions.

Documents commonly translated PL → EN

  • Registry documents — KRS extracts, NIP/REGON certificates, company registration papers
  • Certificates — birth, marriage and death certificates, academic diplomas and transcripts
  • Contracts and agreements — employment contracts, NDAs, commercial agreements under Polish law
  • Financial and tax documents — invoices, tax certificates, annual reports
  • Technical and product documentation — manuals, specifications, datasheets

What makes Polish translation different

Polish is a Slavic language with seven grammatical cases — the same noun changes form depending on its role in the sentence, so word-for-word translation produces confusion. Formal Polish also has formulaic administrative phrasing that has established English equivalents in official contexts.

The real risk areas are names with diacritics (ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż), legal references to Polish statutes, and company identifiers (KRS, NIP, REGON numbers) that must stay exact. DataCompiler flags these passages for human review before delivery.

Scans and paper originals

Polish administrative documents frequently exist only as paper scans, and signed contracts carry handwriting. The pipeline transcribes the source first — preserving diacritics exactly — then translates, so format doesn't matter.
