Translate documents to English

Reading time: 3 min

English is the most requested target language for document translation — contracts, certificates, academic records, invoices and reports all end up needing an English version. Whether your source is French, German, Polish, Chinese or Turkish, the workflow is the same: the document is read, transcribed in its source language, then translated into English.

What changes when translating into English

English translations of European languages typically expand by 10–20% in length, which matters when the translation must fit the original page layout. Legal and fiscal documents need particular care: terms like "joint and several liability" or "value added tax" have exact equivalents that a generic tool may render loosely. Idiomatic source phrasing — common in Italian and Turkish business writing — also requires adaptation rather than literal rendering.

Documents most often translated into English

  • Company registration documents and certificates (KRS, Handelsregister, K-bis equivalents)
  • Contracts, terms of service and NDAs
  • Invoices, tax documents and financial statements
  • Academic diplomas and transcripts
  • Medical and insurance paperwork

How DataCompiler handles English translation

Upload the document and DataCompiler transcribes it in the source language first — essential for scans and handwriting, where translation tools cannot read the file directly — then translates into English. You preview the source text and the English translation side by side before ordering. Passages the engine flags as uncertain are routed to human review, which matters for names, amounts and legal terms.
