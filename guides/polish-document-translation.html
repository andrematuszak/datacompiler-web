Polish document translation

Reading time: 4 min

Poland is one of Europe's fastest-growing nearshoring destinations, and Polish document translation is in constant demand: companies hiring Polish teams need employment contracts translated, Polish subsidiaries send invoices and certificates, and KRS registry documents circulate internationally. DataCompiler translates documents to and from Polish in both directions.

Translating documents into Polish

Companies translating into Polish typically handle HR documents, employment contracts, technical documentation and correspondence with Polish offices. Polish is a Slavic language with seven grammatical cases — nouns change form by role — so word-for-word translation produces confusion. Formal Polish also has formulaic administrative phrasing ("niniejszym", "w nawiązaniu do") that requires correct equivalents.

Polish uses Latin script with diacritics (ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż) that must be preserved exactly — DataCompiler keeps characters like ł intact in rebuilt documents.

Translating Polish documents out

The reverse direction matters just as much: Polish KRS extracts, birth and marriage certificates, academic diplomas, tax documents and contracts under Polish law all need English or other-language versions for banks, regulators, employers and partners.

Scans, handwriting and mixed documents

Polish administrative documents frequently exist only as paper scans, and signed contracts carry handwriting. DataCompiler transcribes the source text first — preserving all diacritics — then translates. Uncertain passages are flagged for human review, which matters for names, amounts and legal references.
