Translate German documents

Reading time: 3 min

Germany produces enormous volumes of formal documents — commercial register extracts, contracts under German law, certificates, invoices and official correspondence — that regularly need translation for international use. DataCompiler translates German documents into English, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for with German source documents

German is the longest of the supported languages relative to English — compounds and formal constructions mean a German page often holds less information than its English equivalent. Legal German is formulaic ("hiermit", "vorsorglich", "in vorbezeichneter Hinsicht") and must be rendered into correct equivalents, not paraphrased. Handwriting on German documents — common on signed contracts and official forms — is a particular challenge that benefits from human review.

Common German documents translated

  • Handelsregister extracts and company documents
  • Contracts governed by German law
  • Certificates: birth, marriage, academic, trade
  • Invoices and financial statements (German "GoBD" requirements make these documents dense)
  • Employment documents and work contracts
