Reading time: 3 min

Turkey is a major trading partner across Europe and Central Asia, and document translation into Turkish is needed for trade documents, contracts, certificates and official paperwork. DataCompiler translates into Turkish from English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for in Turkish document translation

Turkish is an agglutinative language — words are built by chaining suffixes, so a single English word can become a long Turkish compound. Machine translation can produce correct but stilted phrasing; legal and administrative Turkish has its own formulaic conventions ("işbu", "ekli", "yukarıda") that require care.

Turkish uses Latin script with dotted/dotless i distinction (İ/i vs I/ı) — a classic machine-translation bug source. Formal documents also follow strict capitalization rules that tools frequently get wrong.

Common documents translated into Turkish

  • Trade documents: invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin
  • Contracts and partnership agreements
  • Official documents: residence permits, tax filings, notarized papers
  • Certificates: birth, marriage, academic, vaccination
  • Technical manuals and product documentation
