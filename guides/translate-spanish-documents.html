Translate Spanish documents

Reading time: 3 min

Spanish-language documents arrive from Spain and Latin America in equal measure: contracts, certificates, academic records, invoices and official paperwork. One nuance to flag early — European Spanish and Latin American Spanish differ in vocabulary and conventions, so tell the service which variant your document uses if it matters for the target audience.

What to watch for with Spanish source documents

Spanish documents often mix formal administrative language ("comparece", "otorga", "en su calidad de") that needs precise legal equivalents. Handwritten signatures and annotations on scanned Spanish contracts are common and benefit from the human-review step. Dates and number formats also vary: "1.234,56 €" (European) vs "1,234.56" (Latin American) — amounts must survive translation intact.

Common Spanish documents translated

  • Contracts and powers of attorney (very common in Latin American jurisdictions)
  • Birth, marriage and civil registry certificates
  • Academic diplomas and transcripts
  • Invoices, tax documents (Spanish "autónomos" and company filings)
  • Immigration and residency documents
