Translate documents to French

Reading time: 3 min

French is a top-three target language for document translation in Europe: contracts with French partners, official documents for administration, invoices from French suppliers and academic records all need accurate French versions. DataCompiler translates into French from English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for in French document translation

French has two pitfalls for machine translation. First, formal register: administrative and legal French uses the vouvoiement and impersonal constructions — a text translated with an informal "tu" register will look unprofessional. Second, typography: French requires non-breaking spaces before certain punctuation (colons, semicolons, exclamation and question marks, guillemets « »), which naive tools often miss.

Length is also a factor: French typically runs 15–20% longer than English, which affects how the translation fits the original document layout.

Common documents translated into French

  • Commercial contracts and terms of service
  • Invoices, credit notes and payment documents
  • Administrative documents (birth certificates, residency permits, official forms)
  • Technical documentation and user manuals
  • Academic transcripts and diplomas
