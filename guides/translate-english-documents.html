Translate English documents

Reading time: 3 min

English documents need translating in both directions of business: European teams translating English contracts and reports inward, and English-speaking teams sending documents outward. DataCompiler translates English documents into French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese (Simplified).

What to watch for when the source is English

English source text is usually the easiest input for OCR and transcription — clean fonts, predictable spacing — but the target language creates the challenges. Translations into German, French and Polish typically expand 15–25%, which affects layout. English idioms ("terms and conditions", "hereby", "without prejudice") also need correct legal equivalents in the target language, not literal renderings.

When the document contains English legal boilerplate, ask whether the target version should keep English defined terms or translate them — conventions differ by jurisdiction.

Common English documents translated

  • Contracts, NDAs and terms of service
  • Technical documentation and manuals
  • Company documents: certificates of incorporation, board resolutions
  • Academic records and diplomas
  • Immigration and visa paperwork
