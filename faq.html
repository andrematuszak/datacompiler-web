Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about uploading, pricing, languages and how your documents are handled.

What file formats can I upload?

PDF, JPG and PNG files up to 100 MB. Scans, photos and native PDFs are all supported.

How much does transcription and translation cost?

Transcription starts at €0.02 per word and translation at €0.05 per word, adjusted for document difficulty. The exact price appears as soon as you upload your file.

How long does it take?

The delivery date is estimated when you upload, based on the word count — roughly one working day per 2,500 words.

Which languages are supported?

Chinese (Simplified), French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Turkish — in both directions.

What if part of my scan is unclear?

Passages the engine flags as uncertain are routed to human review before delivery, so doubtful sections are verified rather than guessed.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to get a preview, price and delivery estimate — upload a file and see the result immediately.

What happens to my documents?

Files are processed to produce your transcription and translation, then delivered as downloadable PDF and text artifacts. Contact us for data-retention details specific to your needs.
