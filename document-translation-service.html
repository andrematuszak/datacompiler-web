Frequently asked questions

What types of documents can you translate?

PDFs, scans, photos and handwritten documents — contracts, invoices, certificates, reports and correspondence. Files that break generic translation tools work because the pipeline transcribes the source text first.

How fast is the turnaround?

The delivery date is estimated when you upload, based on word count — roughly one working day per 2,500 words. You see the estimate before ordering.

Do you handle recurring or bulk volumes?

Yes — pricing is per word with no minimum commitment, so occasional documents and recurring volumes are priced the same way. A monthly plan for teams is coming soon.

Which languages do you support?

Chinese (Simplified), French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Turkish — in both directions.
